Why I built this
Every parent I know is quietly running a job nobody applied for.
Chief of staff for their own family.
The reality
I was too. Toddlers, a renovation, a full-time job — one evening in the kitchen I remember thinking: there has to be a better way to hold all this.
The realisation
You can't hand off what's only living in your head.
The mental load stays invisible until someone actually writes it down. The moment it's written down, it stops being a feeling — and becomes a list you can delegate.
The product
So I built GiGi.
One quiet update a day. She sees what needs managing across the household — bills, school forms, passports, the weekly shop — and handles it on your behalf, with your approval at every step.
I built it selfishly. I needed it. But if it lifts even a little of that weight off other parents in the same juggle, that's the whole reason I'm sharing it.
Kerstin Skjefstad Larsson
Founder, GiGi
