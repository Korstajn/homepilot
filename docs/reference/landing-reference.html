What accounts does GiGi need?
+
Your email and calendar, read-only. GiGi never sends, posts, or changes anything without your approval. Disconnect any time.
How does bill switching work?
+
GiGi watches your renewal dates, finds a better deal, and asks you to approve before switching. We take 10% of the saving — you keep the rest.
What do you do with my data?
+
Stored securely in the UK, used only to run GiGi. Never sold. Delete everything, anytime, by emailing contact@getgigiapp.com.
Is GiGi right for me?
+
Best for busy households — families with children, dual-income couples, anyone whose admin takes up more headspace than it should.
